Short description

Wardlight is a city builder and tower defence for iPhone and iPad. By day you build only where your light reaches; by night thousands of Blight crawl out of the Mist toward whatever burns brightest. A run is twelve nights and takes about forty minutes. Free to play, one purchase for the full game, no ads.

Long description

You are the Wardlight, the flame at the heart of a village on the edge of an endless Mist. You can only build where your light reaches, and your towers only see lit ground, so a bigger light means a bigger village and a brighter village draws more of the Blight.

The day waits for the player: houses, fields, walls and towers are paid for with the village's supplies, and the night starts when the player calls it. At night the Blight come in surges, up to 2,500 alive at once. Towers fight what the light shows, and the light makes mana for spells and mage towers. Knowledge from the night buys talents on a tree for every building, and at its fourth level the Wardlight takes an element, Ember, Verdant or Dawn, that colours every tower and spell for the rest of the run.

Six lands, each with its own Blight, seasons and a boss on the twelfth night, open one after another. The story is told by an old dwarf lampwright who would rather be retired, against Lady Vaelith, who spreads the Mist so that no light can fall on the tiny scar on her cheek.

The game runs on a renderer written for it in Apple's Metal, with a volumetric Mist and every Blight simulated on the device twenty times a second. It plays fully offline, has no account and shows no ads.
