The mage towers carry the late nights: their magic ignores armour, but every cast takes mana from the pool, and an empty pool silences them. They are late trees, opened at Wardlight II.

**How to read the numbers.** Costs are the spring price in supplies. Every building and every growth step costs ×1 in spring, ×1.2 in summer, ×1.5 in autumn and ×2 in winter, and 5 % more for every copy of it already standing (walls, trenches and caltrops never count). A build cell is 2 m. A building without its own health number has 150. Talents cost Knowledge only, never supplies. On a building's card, **Level 2** costs 50 % of the building's price and makes it do 25 % more; growing it into a specialisation or keystone costs the difference to that building's price, at least 50 % of its own. Damage a second is worked out from the damage and the rate, into one target before armour.

In this page: Fireball tower, Lightning tower, Hex tower, Summoning circle.

## Fireball tower

**A late tree:** learning it costs 10 Knowledge and needs Wardlight II; its specialisations need III and its keystones IV.

Lobs fireballs that set crowds Burning.

- **On hit:** Leaves the Blight it hits Burning 3 s.
- **Mana:** Every cast takes mana, and an empty pool silences it.
- **Armour:** Ignores armour every night.

- **Strong against:** Brutes and Bloaters: fire goes through armour, and a Burning Bloater bursts harmless.
- **Weak against:** Hexwards and Husk Knights: their mantles swallow most of each fireball.
- **Tip:** Build it where your light reaches the crowd.
- **Tip:** It aims at Oiled Blight first.
- **Advisor's tip:** It burns through armour but eats mana every cast. Dig an oil trench in front of it, and it picks the Oiled ones first.

**Base talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Wildfire** (10 Knowledge): Passes Burning on to the Blight beside one that dies.
- **High arc** (10 Knowledge): Gives +5 m range. *Passive:* Aims first at the Blight striking a wall or building.
- **Kindling** (10 Knowledge): Lights the oil and trenches each fireball lands near. *Chance:* Gives every tower of the fireball tree +15 % chance to set off Ignite.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at III, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built fireball tower at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Pyre tower (specialisation)

Throws a huge fireball that knocks the Blight back.

- **On hit:** Throws the Blight 2 m back and leaves it Burning 3 s.
- **Ignite:** 60 % chance to set an Oiled Blight alight: the flare jumps to every Oiled Blight near it.
- **Mana:** Every cast takes mana, and an empty pool silences it.

- **Strong against:** Crowds at the wall: it throws them 2 m back and sets them Burning.
- **Weak against:** Manaleeches: every hit drains your pool, and each cast already costs 32.
- **Advisor's tip:** It throws them 2 m back, which buys your wall time. Each cast costs 32 mana, so keep a shrine or two working for it.

Needs 2 of the Pyre tower's talents and Wardlight IV; learning it costs 45 Knowledge, and one pyre tower then grows into it on its card. One per village. A **global** keystone: it has its own effect only, and boosts no other building.

Brings a sun down on the thickest Blight, all night long.

- **Weak against:** Hexwards and Husk Knights: their mantles swallow most of the blast.
- **Advisor's tip:** It strikes the thickest crowd you can see, anywhere on the map. Light more of the Mist and it finds more to hit.

## Lightning tower

**A late tree:** learning it costs 10 Knowledge and needs Wardlight II; its specialisations need III and its keystones IV.

Fires a bolt that jumps from Blight to Blight.

- **Chain:** Jumps to 1 more Blight within 3 m. With none there, it strikes the same one again for half.
- **Mana:** Every cast takes mana, and an empty pool silences it.
- **Armour:** Ignores armour every night.

- **Strong against:** Wisps and Shades: lightning hits them harder, and it jumps on.
- **Weak against:** Hexwards and Manaleeches: one swallows the bolt, the other drinks your mana.
- **Tip:** Build it where your light reaches the crowd.
- **Advisor's tip:** The bolt jumps to one more Blight, so it likes them close together. I'd build it for Wisps, and for Shades once you can see them.

A lightning tower whose bolt runs through a whole swarm.

- **Advisor's tip:** It jumps through 6 more Blight, and a jump through a Wet one costs nothing. A bog field in front of it does the wetting.

## Hex tower

**A late tree:** learning it costs 10 Knowledge and needs Wardlight II; its specialisations need III and its keystones IV.

Poisons the Blight from 23.5 m, and the poison eats through armour and slows it.

- **On hit:** Puts 3 poison stacks on the Blight it hits. Each takes 0.6 % of its health a second through armour and slows it 3 %, up to 45 %.
- **Stacks:** Hits 10 % harder for every poison stack the Blight already carries. A slain one passes half its stacks on.
- **Mana:** Every cast takes mana, and an empty pool silences it.

- **Strong against:** Brutes and the Shellback: its poison goes through armour and piles up with every hit.
- **Weak against:** Hexwards and Manaleeches: one swallows the hex, the other drinks your mana.
- **Advisor's tip:** Its poison eats through armour and piles up with every hit, so Brutes hate it. Put a volley or repeater tower in its reach and the poison spreads.

### Ice tower (specialisation)

Leaves frost that slows the Blight, then freezes it.

- **Ground:** Leaves frost within 5 m of where it lands for 5 s. It Slows the Blight in it.
- **Frozen:** Freezes a Blight for 2.5 s on its fourth hit, or on its second if it is Wet.
- **Shatter:** Each Blight it freezes calls the nearest mangonel or ballista in reach to shoot it at once.

- **Strong against:** Crowds and the Cinder Stag: frost Slows them, and cold counts twice on a Wet one.
- **Weak against:** Hexwards and Husk Knights: their mantles swallow most of the frost.
- **Tip:** A Blight Frozen 2 times in 15 s is Frost-hardened for 8 s. Let fire or stones finish it.
- **Tip:** A boss needs twice the hits, stays Frozen half as long and is Frost-hardened at once.
- **Advisor's tip:** It freezes a Blight on the fourth hit, on the second if it's Wet. The nearest mangonel or ballista then shoots at once and shatters it.
