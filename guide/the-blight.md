The Blight comes out of the Mist every night and marches to the nearest light, breaking whatever blocks its way, toward the Wardlight. Each night has a budget in **Blight points (BP)**; each kind costs its BP, so the budget buys many cheap Blight or fewer big ones.

## How damage works

- **Armour** takes that much off every hit of arrows, stones and other plain damage (at least 1 stays). Magic (the mage towers), Burning, poison, smites and the Wardlight's spells ignore it. Once the Wardlight is attuned, arrows and stones go through 40 % of it. In winter every Blight has +2 armour.
- **Shroud** blunts magic, the spells included, by its share.
- **Mantle** (the magic-proof kinds): swallows that share of every mage tower's hit. Spells go through it.
- **Weaknesses:** some kinds take more (or less) from a damage family: arrows (watchtowers, ballistas and their lines, and walls that strike back), stones (mangonels), fire, magic, light (the Wardlight, wards, lanterns, prisms, flares and the shrines' fighters), traps and rallied villagers.
- **Sight:** towers shoot only what they see: Blight in light of 0.3 or more, or Revealed.
- **Bosses** take a quarter of every synergy event's damage ([synergies](/guide/states-and-synergies/)).

## Which kinds come in a run

Every run brings Crawlers and exactly one kind from every bucket the player's unlock tier opens, drawn when the run is made. A land's favourites are ×2 as likely within their bucket. No other kind comes during the run. The year ahead shows the run's kinds before the first day.

| Bucket | Kinds | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Besiegers | Bloater and Spitter | Spitter tier 3 |
| Breakers | Brute and Burrower | open from the start |
| Light hunters | Wisp and Shade | open from the start |
| Magic-proof | Hexward, Manaleech and Husk Knight | Hexward tier 2; Manaleech tier 2; Husk Knight tier 2 |

The three base buckets' kinds come in a drawn order from nights 2, 5 and 7; the magic-proof kind from night 6. Each kind that has come takes a share of the night's budget: 12 % from night 1, 22 % from night 4, 20 % from night 5, 18 % from night 6, 17 % from night 7, 18 % from night 8, 20 % from night 9, together at most 80 %; the Crawlers fill the rest. On night 10 the land's favourites in the run come with ×1.5 health. A land's favourite comes as the land's own variant (see [the lands](/guide/lands/)).

### Crawler

Comes in crowds down the paths to your light.

- **Goes for:** the nearest light, breaking what blocks its way.

- **At the wall:** Hits whatever blocks its path until it breaks.
- **From night 7:** Grows armour that soaks up arrows and stones.

- **Tip:** Hold them at a palisade within a watchtower's reach.
- **Tip:** Have a fireball tower up by night 7.

### Wisp

Flies high over the crowd, from light to light, to your Wardlight.

- **Goes for:** wards, then lights.
- **Flies:** over walls and other Blight.
- **Takes:** ×1.5 from arrows, ×1 from stones, ×0.6 from fire, ×1.25 from magic (mage towers), ×1.5 from light, ×0 from traps, ×0 from rallied villagers.

- **Dive:** Comes down for the last 4 m, and only then do stones and fire reach it.
- **Burst:** Takes 12 mana and puts the light out for 3 s. After 3 at one light, the rest of the swarm flies on toward the Wardlight.
- **At the Wardlight:** Each one that reaches it bursts there, striking it for 20 and taking 12 mana.

- **Tip:** Stand watchtowers and lightning towers by the lights between the Mist and the Wardlight.
- **Tip:** Keep 12 mana in the pool, or each light it puts out stays dark for 10 s.

### Spitter

Stops short of your wall and spits at it.

- **Goes for:** walls, then towers.
- **Unlock tier 3:** it never comes before then.
- **Takes:** ×1.5 from arrows, ×0.6 from stones, ×1.25 from light, ×0 from traps, ×1.5 from rallied villagers.

- **No wall near:** Spits at a tower instead.
- **Spit:** Hits harder with each spit while it stands: 3 at first, up to 10 after 14 s. Moving starts it over.
- **On hit:** Each spit Reveals it for a moment.

- **Tip:** Keep watchtowers right behind the wall.
- **Tip:** Send the Rally out to it.

### Bloater

Waddles to your wall, swollen with poison, and bursts there.

- **Goes for:** walls.
- **Takes:** ×1.25 from arrows, ×1.5 from fire, ×0.6 from traps.

- **Burst:** Deals 160 to every wall and building within 3 m. A palisade breaks.
- **Gas:** If it's Burning when it dies, it bursts without harm.

- **Tip:** Set it Burning before it reaches the wall. Fireball towers do that.

### Brute

Goes for your gate first, then walls and towers.

- **Goes for:** gates, then walls, then towers.

- **Blow:** Breaks a palisade in 7.5 s.
- **From night 6:** Its armour doubles and arrows barely scratch it.
- **In a pit:** Breaks free of spike pits after 1 s.

- **Tip:** Put a fireball tower behind your gate. Magic goes through its armour.

### Burrower

Digs under your walls and comes up inside your light.

- **Goes for:** workplaces, then houses.
- **Burrows:** tunnels under walls.

- **Tunnel:** Moves at half speed underground, where nothing sees it.
- **When it surfaces:** Goes for your workplaces and houses.

- **Tip:** Keep a tower near your houses too.
- **Tip:** Learn stone floors and it can't dig into a house.

### Shade

Slips past your walls to hunt your towers.

- **Goes for:** towers.
- **Shroud:** unseen even in light until Revealed or in light of 0.45 or more.
- **Takes:** ×1.25 from arrows, ×1.25 from magic (mage towers), ×2 from light.

- **Shroud:** Stays unseen in the Mist and at the dim edge of your light.
- **Revealed:** Takes twice the damage from the Wardlight and your wards.

- **Tip:** Put a watch lantern by your towers to Reveal it within 8 m.
- **Counters:** Watch lantern and Ward.

### Hexward

Walks to your towers under a rune mantle.

- **Goes for:** towers.
- **Mantle:** swallows 80 % of every mage tower's hit.
- **Magic-proof:** answer it with plain towers.
- **Unlock tier 2:** it never comes before then.
- **Takes:** ×1.25 from arrows, ×1.25 from stones.

- **Mantle:** Swallows 80 % of every mage tower's hit.

- **Tip:** Cover your mage towers with watchtowers and mangonels.
- **Tip:** Your spells go through its mantle.

### Manaleech

Drinks from your mana pool whenever a mage tower hits it.

- **Goes for:** wards, then lights.
- **Magic-proof:** answer it with plain towers.
- **Unlock tier 2:** it never comes before then.
- **Takes:** ×1.25 from arrows, ×1.25 from traps.

- **Mana pool:** Each mage tower's hit on it takes 1 mana from your pool for every 10 damage.

- **Tip:** Kill it with watchtowers before your mage towers waste mana on it.

### Husk Knight

Empty plate that walks to your towers, and magic falls silent round it.

- **Goes for:** towers, then gates, then walls.
- **Mantle:** swallows 70 % of every mage tower's hit.
- **Magic-proof:** answer it with plain towers.
- **Unlock tier 2:** it never comes before then.
- **Takes:** ×1.5 from stones, ×1.25 from rallied villagers.

- **Mantle:** Swallows 70 % of every mage tower's hit.
- **Nearby:** Mage towers within 8 m can't cast, and not for 5 s after.

- **Tip:** Meet it with mangonels. Stones break its plates.
- **Tip:** Keep your mage towers 8 m behind the wall.

## Bosses and elites

A boss is paid from its night's budget and comes with the last surge unless noted. It announces itself the dawn before, shows a boss bar while it lives, and pays a reward when slain (the final bosses' reward is the night itself). Between the boss nights an earlier boss comes back as an **elite**, from the night's busiest side, with a smaller reward and no Feat.

| Night | Boss | Health | Surge | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Young ram (elite) | 440 | the last | 6 supplies and 4 Knowledge |
| 4 | Iron Ram | 1,100 | the last | 30 supplies and 20 Knowledge |
| 6 | Iron Ram (elite) | 1,100 | the last | 15 supplies and 10 Knowledge |
| 8 | Shellback | 3,000 | the first | 20 supplies and a free spell offer |
| 10 | Shellback (elite) | 1,800 | the first | 6 supplies |
| 12 | The land's final boss | see below | the last | the win |

### Iron Ram

Walks one straight line to the Wardlight and breaks all that stands on it.

**Numbers:** 1,100 health, 250 a blow, 0.4 blows a second, 1 m/s, armour 2, 75 to buildings that aren't walls.

- **At dusk:** Marks its line on the ground.
- **Blow:** Breaks a palisade or timber wall at once, a stone wall in two.
- **When slain:** Pays 30 supplies and 20 Knowledge.

- **Tip:** Line its path with watchtowers.
- **Tip:** Build nothing on its line.

### Shellback

Comes with the first surge, and the Blight walk sheltered under its shell.

**Numbers:** 3,000 health, 110 a blow, 0.5 blows a second, 1 m/s, armour 40.

- **Plates:** Takes 0.2 from each arrow or stone.
- **Shell:** Gives Blight within 7 m extra armour, and magic hurts them less.
- **When slain:** Pays 20 supplies, and a spell offer at the next dawn if a spell slot is free.

- **Tip:** Mage towers ignore its armour. Aim them at it first.
- **Tip:** Kill it before the later surges walk under its shell.

### Mother Below

Night 12's boss on the Witchwood. Tunnels under your village and breaks buildings where she rises.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 50 a blow, 1 blows a second, 1.2 m/s, armour 4, armour 20 on night 12, shroud 25 %.

- **When it surfaces:** Breaks the marked buildings in the red ring until dawn, 1 at first, 2 below 66 % health and 4 below 33 %, and every wall in it.
- **Underground:** Cracks the ground in a red ring 9 s before it comes up, and digs down again after 12 s.
- **At a quarter:** Screams, and every Blight runs faster for 8 s.

- **Tip:** Cast Bulwark on the red ring, and the buildings and walls in it stand.
- **Tip:** Hit her while she is up, since nothing reaches her underground.

### Drowned King

Night 12's boss on the Fenlands. Wades the deep mere where no ford runs, and puts out every fire near it.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 150 a blow, 0.5 blows a second, 0.9 m/s, armour 4, armour 20 on night 12, 50 to buildings that aren't walls.

- **At dusk:** Marks its line across the water to the Wardlight.
- **Nearby:** Every Blight within 6 m is Wet, and no fire touches it.
- **Burst:** Bursts at 66 % and 33 % health and lets out 3 Bloaters.

- **Tip:** Meet it with lightning towers, whose strikes run through its Wet crowd.
- **Tip:** Build nothing on its line across the water.
- **Counters:** Lightning tower.

### Mountain's Fist

Night 12's boss on the Highlands. Climbs the ridge where no pass runs and tears a way through it.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 190 a blow, 0.4 blows a second, 1 m/s, armour 12, 100 to buildings that aren't walls.

**Takes:** ×1.5 from stones.

- **Ridge:** Brings the scree down as it climbs, and the Blight walk the new way until dawn.
- **Throw:** Throws a rock at a tower within 24 m every 8 s, after a wind-up.
- **Frozen:** Stands still and throws nothing.

- **Tip:** Meet it with mangonels and silver ballistas, whose stones and bolts break it.
- **Tip:** Wall the ridge on its line, where the scree will fall.
- **Counters:** Mangonel and Silver ballista.

### Cinder Stag

Night 12's boss on the Ashwood. Bounds through your village, burning, and sets alight all it passes.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 75 a blow, 0.8 blows a second, 1.8 m/s, armour 8, 50 to buildings that aren't walls.

**Takes:** ×0 from fire.

- **Burning:** Fire can't hurt it, and every other hit keeps 40 %.
- **Fire:** Sets alight every building within 2.5 m and leaves burning ground behind it.
- **Wet:** Puts it out for 5 s, and an ice tower then freezes it at once.

- **Tip:** Lay spike pits in its way, since their Wet puts it out.
- **Tip:** Keep an ice tower by the pits to freeze it while it's out.
- **Counters:** Spike pits and Ice tower.

### The Glass Bearer

Night 12's boss on the Stonehold. Comes up your oldest shaft, behind every tower, bearing a glass no shot can pass.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 150 a blow, 0.4 blows a second, 0.7 m/s, armour 4, 50 to buildings that aren't walls.

- **At dusk:** Marks its line from your oldest shaft to the Wardlight.
- **Glass:** Turns every tower's shot that comes from the Wardlight's side.
- **Cracked:** Turns nothing once mangonel stones have broken the glass.

- **Tip:** Build towers beside your oldest shaft, where they reach its back.
- **Tip:** Keep a mangonel in reach of the shaft. Only a stone breaks a glass.
- **Counters:** Mangonel.

### The Thornless

Night 12's boss on the Rosegarth. Cuts his own way through your hedges, and the gate you kept open grows shut behind him.

**Numbers:** 6,000 health, 190 a blow, 0.5 blows a second, 0.8 m/s, armour 6, 50 to buildings that aren't walls.

**Takes:** ×1.5 from fire.

- **Cut:** Cuts a gap in a hedge in 4 s, and the Blight walk through it until dawn.
- **Grown shut:** Closes the nearest open gate after each cut until fire touches it.
- **Felling:** At 75, 50 and 25 % health he swings for 3 s and fells 12 m of hedge on each of three sides.

- **Tip:** Build a fireball tower in reach of the gate you hold. Its fire burns the roses open.
- **Tip:** Freeze him with an ice tower. Frozen, he neither cuts nor fells.
- **Counters:** Fireball tower and Ice tower.

The Mother Below is the Witchwood's final boss; every other land sends its own (see [the lands](/guide/lands/)).
