Wardlight is a colony-survival city builder for iPhone and iPad. You are the Wardlight, the spirit in a village's heart crystal: build inside your light by day, defend it from the Blight at night, for twelve nights. This guide explains every system and lists the numbers the game uses, so you (or an assistant you ask) can answer questions such as what a building costs, what beats a kind of Blight, or how a synergy works.

## [How a run works](/guide/how-a-run-works/)

## [The Wardlight](/guide/the-wardlight/)

The Wardlight itself: its health, light and mana, how it strikes back, its four levels, the spells and their attuned forms, the three Attunements and their signature spells, and the Dares.

Houses, workplaces, libraries and shrines, and each land's own building: what they cost, what they pay, and every talent, specialisation and keystone in their trees.

## [Buildings: magic](/guide/magic-buildings/)

## About the numbers

Every number here is generated from the game's data as of 2026-11-23. Balance changes between versions, so a number in your game may differ after an update. Where the guide says "night 5", it counts the run's nights from 1 to 12.
