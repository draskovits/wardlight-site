Defence buildings are the early game: arrows, stones, bolts, traps and walls. They cost nothing per shot, but armour blunts them as the nights go on.

**How to read the numbers.** Costs are the spring price in supplies. Every building and every growth step costs ×1 in spring, ×1.2 in summer, ×1.5 in autumn and ×2 in winter, and 5 % more for every copy of it already standing (walls, trenches and caltrops never count). A build cell is 2 m. A building without its own health number has 150. Talents cost Knowledge only, never supplies. On a building's card, **Level 2** costs 50 % of the building's price and makes it do 25 % more; growing it into a specialisation or keystone costs the difference to that building's price, at least 50 % of its own. Damage a second is worked out from the damage and the rate, into one target before armour.

In this page: Watchtower, Mangonel, Silver ballista, Spike pits, Palisade.

## Watchtower

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Shoots the nearest Blight, flying Wisps too.

- **Splash:** Each arrow also hits everything within 1 m of its target for half.

- **Strong against:** Wisps and Spitters: arrows hit them half again as hard, high up or far out.
- **Weak against:** Brutes, Husk Knights and the Shellback: armour soaks up most of each arrow.
- **Tip:** Build it behind a wall, where your light reaches past it.
- **Advisor's tip:** The first tower I'd build and the last I'd tear down. Set it behind a wall and leave the Brutes to your mage towers.

- **Fire arrows** (10 Knowledge): Sets every Blight it hits Burning and lights the oil trench under it. *Ignite:* Lights an Oiled Blight's flare 50 % of the time, 75 % from volley and repeater towers. *Spread:* Makes volley and repeater towers spread poison 75 % of the time, not 60 %.
- **High perch** (10 Knowledge): Gives a watchtower on rock or beside a wall +4 m range.
- **Full quivers** (10 Knowledge): Shoots twice as fast for its first 6 s of every surge.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built watchtower at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Volley tower (specialisation)

Rains arrows on a circle round its target.

- **Splash:** Hits everything in the splash, so a crowd takes the most.
- **Spread:** 60 % chance its rain splashes a Poisoned Blight's poison onto those beside it.

- **Strong against:** Crawler crowds: its rain hits every one under it.
- **Weak against:** Brutes and the Shellback: armour soaks up most of each small hit.
- **Advisor's tip:** Its rain hits every Crawler under it. Put it in a hex tower's reach, and every volley carries the poison on.

## Mangonel

**Learn it** for 10 Knowledge to put it on the build bar.

Lobs stones into crowds of Blight.

- **Splash:** Hits everything in the splash, so a crowd takes the most.
- **Shatter:** 35 % chance to shatter a Frozen Blight for 4× the stone through armour, and the shards hit the Blight behind it.

- **Strong against:** Crawler crowds and Husk Knights: the splash hits the whole crowd, and stones break plate.
- **Weak against:** Wisps and Spitters: it hits Wisps only in their dive, and Spitters shrug off stones.
- **Tip:** Set it well back from the wall, so the crowd at the wall is in reach.
- **Tip:** Shatter them early. A Blight Frozen 2 times in 15 s is Frost-hardened for 8 s.
- **Advisor's tip:** Stones are for crowds, and crowds gather at your wall. Set it well back from the wall, so the crowd there is in its reach.

- **Spotter crew** (10 Knowledge): Aims at the biggest group in reach instead of the nearest Blight.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built mangonel at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Fire-pot mangonel (specialisation)

A mangonel that throws pots of burning oil.

- **On hit:** Sets everything in the splash Burning.
- **Oil:** Lights any oil trench it hits.
- **Ignite:** 60 % chance to set the Oiled Blight in the splash alight, and the flare jumps on.

- **Strong against:** Bloaters and crowds: the whole splash burns, and a Burning Bloater bursts harmless.
- **Weak against:** Wisps: they fly over the pots, and fire hurts them less.
- **Advisor's tip:** It lights any oil trench it hits. Dig one in front of the wall, and the first pot turns it into an Inferno.

## Silver ballista

**Learn it** for 10 Knowledge to put it on the build bar. It waits for unlock tier 2 (see [unlock tiers](/guide/memory-and-difficulty/)).

Shoots heavy bolts at the strongest Blight, then at the magic-proof.

- **Runebreak:** Hits a Hexward for double and strips its mantle for 4 s.
- **Harvest:** Each Blight it slays gives 1 mana, up to 40 a night.
- **Passive:** Aims at bosses, Brutes and Husk Knights first, then the Frozen, then the magic-proof.

- **Strong against:** Hexwards, Brutes and Husk Knights: it aims at them first, and a Hexward takes double.
- **Weak against:** Crawler crowds: one bolt, one target, every 1.3 s.
- **Tip:** Stand it near your mage towers, where Hexwards walk to.
- **Advisor's tip:** It goes for the big ones first and strips a Hexward's mantle. Stand it near your mage towers, so they hit the Hexward while it's bare.

## Spike pits

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Leaves the Blight that steps in Wet and Slowed, and wounds it.

- **Catch:** Leaves a Blight Slowed 3 s and deals 10, up to 10 times a night.
- **Bait:** Bait it by day for one kind, and it catches that kind first and hurts it more.
- **Brutes:** Brutes and the Mother Below stay Slowed only 1 s.

- **Strong against:** Crawlers and the kind you bait: each catch leaves them Wet and Slowed.
- **Weak against:** Wisps, Spitters and Brutes: Wisps fly over, Spitters stop short, Brutes break free in 1 s.
- **Tip:** Dig pits beside a lantern for a Lantern snare.
- **Advisor's tip:** Every catch leaves the Blight Wet, 10 a night. Bait them for the kind you fear tonight and keep a lightning tower in reach.

- **Rearm** (10 Knowledge): Resets pits after each surge, up to 16 catches a night. *Called early:* Each surge you call early gives every pit 1 more catch at each reset until dawn.
- **Deep pits** (10 Knowledge): Keeps the Blight a pit catches Slowed 1 s longer.
- **Fresh bait** (10 Knowledge): Makes a baited pit lure its kind of Blight from 10 m, not 6, and hold 2 more catches a night. Tip: Bait a pit by day on its card, for the kind you fear most.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built spike pits at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Oil trench (specialisation)

Soaks the Blight in oil for your fire to light.

- **Oiled:** Leaves the Blight that cross it Oiled 5 s. Fire can burn the oil off in a flare that jumps on.
- **Inferno:** Any fire turns it into an Inferno that burns 8 s for 3 times the damage.
- **At dusk:** Fills up again for free.

- **Strong against:** Crowds in front of your fire towers: the oil turns any flame into an Inferno.
- **Weak against:** Wisps: they fly over it and never get Oiled.
- **Tip:** Light it with a fire-pot mangonel.
- **Advisor's tip:** Oil does nothing until somebody brings a flame. Any fire turns it into an Inferno, and it fills up again for free at dusk.

| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Oil | Oiled 5 s; once lit it burns 8 s for ×3 damage and jumps 2 m |

**Talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Thick pitch** (20 Knowledge): Slows the Blight in a burning oil trench by 60 %, so it burns longer.
- **Fire ditch** (20 Knowledge): Lights itself once a surge when 6 Blight stand in it.
- **Slick** (20 Knowledge): Slows the Blight in it.

Every oil trench burns longer, its oil clings twice as long, and the fire runs further.

- **Strong against:** Crowds in your oil trenches: they burn half again as long, and the flare jumps 4 m.
- **Weak against:** Wisps: they fly over the trenches.
- **Advisor's tip:** Your trenches burn half again as long, and every flare jumps 4 m. It's worth it once your front is lined with oil trenches.

- **Tip:** Lay it in front of a wall your towers cover.
- **Advisor's tip:** It never runs out and leaves everything Wet and Slowed, Brutes too. Lay it where a lightning tower covers your wall.

## Palisade

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Blocks the Blight's way with a cheap wall of stakes.

- **Gate:** Make any segment a gate so your villagers can get out.
- **Rebuild:** Rebuilds in place as timber, then stone, once you learn them.

- **Strong against:** Crawlers: they stop to hit it, inside your watchtowers' reach.
- **Weak against:** Brutes, Bloaters and the Iron Ram: a Brute breaks it in 7.5 s, a Bloater or the Ram at once.
- **Tip:** Leave a gate toward the forest for your woodcutters and hunters.
- **Advisor's tip:** Stakes stop Crawlers in front of your watchtowers, and not much else. Make one segment a gate toward the forest for the woodcutters.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | 1 supply (spring price, per segment) |
| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Health | 150 |
| Armour | 1 |

**Base talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Stakes** (10 Knowledge): Deals 3 damage to the Blight that hit it. *Crawlers:* Are knocked back and Stunned when they hit it. *Bounty:* Makes tonight's bounty pay 10 % more.
- **Bracing** (10 Knowledge): Gives segments within 4 m of a tower or ward +50 health.
- **Wall ditch** (10 Knowledge): Gives every spike pit within 4 m of this wall 2 more catches a night. Tip: Dig spike pits in front of your wall.

**One long path instead of two specialisations:** palisade → timber wall → stone wall → Bastion. Each step costs 20 Knowledge and rebuilds the whole line in place; its talents (20 each) carry over as the line is rebuilt further. The first step needs 2 base talents and Wardlight II, the next 2 of the step's talents and Wardlight III.

### Timber wall (path step)

Rebuilds the line in timber, mended free each dawn.

- **At dawn:** Repairs to full health for free.
- **Fire:** Catches fire and burns.

- **Strong against:** Crawlers and Spitters: their small hits are repaired free at dawn.
- **Weak against:** The Iron Ram and the Cinder Stag: the Ram breaks it at once, the Stag sets it on fire.
- **Advisor's tip:** It mends free every dawn, so small hits don't add up. The Iron Ram breaks it at once, so build stone on its path.

- **Weak against:** Bloaters and the Iron Ram: a burst takes 160, and the Ram breaks it in two blows.
- **Advisor's tip:** Stone doesn't mend itself at dawn the way timber does. Keep it in a ward's light, and keep Bloaters off it with fire.

#### Bastion (keystone)

- **After a surge:** Patches every wall within 20 m.
- **Nearby:** Towers within 12 m have three times the health.

- **Strong against:** Brutes and Husk Knights: 800 health and armour 16 hold them at the wall.
- **Weak against:** Mother Below: she breaks every wall in her red ring.
- **Advisor's tip:** After each surge it patches every wall within 20 m. Put it where your wall takes the beating and your towers stand close.
