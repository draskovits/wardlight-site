You are the Wardlight, the village's heart crystal. It has no body to move: its magic lands where you tap.

Your village's heart. If it breaks, the run is lost.

- **On hit:** Strikes back at the Blight that hit it with a bolt of light.
- **Burst:** Once a night at 75 %, 50 % and 25 % health, light bursts from the Wardlight: strongest close by, fading out by 24 m. The Blight it reaches is Stunned.
- **At night:** Makes 2 mana a second while the night runs, and 0.25 more for each level you raise it.

- **Tip:** Keep a wall and towers between it and the Mist.
- **Tip:** It heals only 150 at dawn, so raise it with supplies for more health.
- **Advisor's tip:** It heals only 150 at dawn, and the rest of tonight's scratches stay. Raise it while the supplies allow, and stop letting things bite it.

## Levels

Raise the Wardlight on its card for supplies once the village has the villagers, buildings and lights. Buildings count everything but the Wardlight, walls, traps and temporary ones; lights count every ward, lantern, prism and flare post with their specialisations and keystones.

| Level | Supplies | Villagers | Buildings | Lights | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | — | — | — | — | 11 m light, 1,000 health, 2 mana a second; a spell offer |
| II | 20 | 20 | 16 | 3 | +2 m light (13 m); +250 health (1,250); +0.25 mana a second (2.25); the trees' first checkpoints; the mage towers |
| III | 40 | 34 | 38 | 7 | +2 m light (15 m); +250 health (1,500); +0.25 mana a second (2.5); a spell offer; the trees' second checkpoints (the mage towers' first) |
| IV | 70 | 46 | 57 | 11 | +2 m light (17 m); +250 health (1,750); +0.25 mana a second (2.75); the mage towers' second checkpoints; the Attunement |

Each level also makes your spells stronger (×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.75 at I–IV) and your rallied villagers tougher (×1, ×1.5, ×2.5, ×4 health and damage at I–IV).

## Its answer

- **Retaliation:** every blow the Wardlight takes is answered by a bolt of light at the attacker: 3 × the hit + 5 a level, at most 3 bolts a second. It is light: a Shade takes it twice, and it carries the Attunement's tint.
- **The bursts:** the first time in a night its health falls below 75 %, 50 %, 25 %, light bursts from it, once each: 30 / 50 / 75 / 100 damage (by its level I–IV) through armour, full up to 8 m and fading out by 24 m; the Blight it reaches is Stunned 1.5 s, and the Blight within 10 m is thrown back 4 m. Bosses take the damage only. The bursts come back with the next night.
- **Healing:** only 150 at each dawn; the rest of the night's damage stays. Raising it adds health.

## Spells

- **Two spell slots.** At Wardlight I and III the Wardlight offers 3 spells at random; you keep 1. A slain Shellback gives a free offer at the next dawn while a slot is free.
- **Spells cost no mana**, only their cooldown, and recover fully at dawn.
- **Spells ignore armour**, a Hexward's mantle and a Shellback's shell. A Blight's shroud still blunts them.
- **Spell power** multiplies every spell's damage and mending: the Wardlight's level (×(1 + 0.25 × (level − 1))), a staffed Arcanum ×1.2 and a Grand Arcanum ×1.3 more, and the Memory's Spell might. No cooldown falls below 4 s.
- **Attuned forms:** once the Wardlight is attuned, every spell takes the element's form (below).

### Burst

Blasts the Blight round where it lands.

- **Cast:** Hits every Blight in the circle and throws it back 3 m.

- **Tip:** Wait for a crowd to bunch up at a wall, then cast.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Firefall**, leaves burning ground under every Blight it hits; Verdant: **Spore burst**, gives every Blight it hits Rot; Dawn: **Thunderclap**, stuns every Blight it hits, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### Lance

Burns a straight line through the Blight.

- **Cast:** Starts where you tap and runs straight out, away from the Wardlight.

- **Tip:** Tap where a path enters your light, so the line runs down the path.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Flame lance**, leaves burning ground under every Blight it hits; Verdant: **Plague lance**, gives every Blight it hits Rot; Dawn: **Sun spear**, stuns every Blight it hits, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### Oil flood

Floods the ground with oil, then sets it alight.

- **Cast:** Oils and Slows every Blight that walks into the pool.
- **Ignite:** Tap the spell again to set the pool alight for 4 s, free.
- **Spread:** Fire jumps from the blaze to every Oiled Blight near it.

- **Tip:** Lay it where a fireball tower shoots, since any Burning Blight lights the pool.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Naphtha**, lights the pool the moment it lands; Verdant: **Bog**, gives the Blight in the pool Rot instead of Slowing it; Dawn: **Storm oil**, lights the pool when Dawn's lightning strikes in it.

### Bulwark

Mends your walls and buildings, and hardens the walls until dawn.

- **Mend:** Mends every wall and building in the circle.
- **Until dawn:** Gives the walls in the circle +2 armour and +50 % health.

- **Tip:** Cast it on the wall the surge is heading for, before it arrives.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Forge**, leaves burning ground under every Blight in the circle; Verdant: **Overgrowth**, gives every Blight in the circle Rot; Dawn: **Sanctify**, stuns every Blight in the circle, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### Hold

Stops the Blight in a small circle where it stands.

- **Cast:** Stuns every Blight in the circle where it stands.
- **While Stunned:** It can't walk or strike.

- **Tip:** Cast it where your towers already shoot.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Cinder trap**, leaves burning ground under every Blight it Stuns; Verdant: **Grasping roots**, gives every Blight it Stuns Rot; Dawn: **Halo**, lightning jumps from every Blight it Stuns on to 1 more.

### Hoarfrost

Lays frost on the ground that freezes the Blight standing in it.

- **Ground:** Freezes a Blight for 2.5 s once it has stood 4 s in the frost.
- **Cast:** The Blight in the circle when it lands freezes a second sooner.

- **Tip:** Lay it where a stone thrower shoots, since it shatters the Frozen Blight.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Frostburn**, leaves burning ground under every Blight the frost lands on; Verdant: **Black frost**, gives every Blight the frost lands on Rot; Dawn: **Diamond dust**, stuns every Blight the frost lands on, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### Haste

Speeds up the towers round where it lands.

- **Cast:** Doubles how fast every tower in the circle shoots.

- **Tip:** Cast it over your watchtowers, since mage towers don't speed up.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Fury**, their hits leave burning ground; Verdant: **Fever**, their hits give Rot; Dawn: **Zeal**, their hits Stun, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### Siphon

Draws the life out of the Blight in a circle, as mana.

- **Mana:** Gives you 3 mana for every Blight it hits.

- **Tip:** Cast it on the thickest crowd you can find.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Cinder siphon**, leaves burning ground under every Blight it hits; Verdant: **Leech**, gives every Blight it hits Rot; Dawn: **Tithe**, stuns every Blight it hits, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

### False light

Lights a lure in the dark that draws the Blight, then bursts.

- **Lure:** Turns every Blight on foot in the circle toward the false light.
- **Burst:** Bursts when the lure ends and hits every Blight close by.

- **Tip:** Drop it where your towers can shoot the crowd it gathers.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Bonfire**, leaves burning ground under every Blight it lures and bursts on; Verdant: **Carrion**, gives every Blight it lures and bursts on Rot; Dawn: **Mirage**, stuns every Blight it lures and bursts on.

### Starfall

Only in the offers once the Memory's Starfall star is lit (40 Echoes).

Pulls stars down on a wide circle, one after another.

- **Cast:** Drops 12 stars one after another at random in the circle.
- **Each star:** Hits every Blight right where it lands.

- **Tip:** Cast it on a crowd stopped at a wall, where few stars miss.

**Attuned:** Ember: **Meteor shower**, leaves burning ground under every Blight a star hits; Verdant: **Spore rain**, gives every Blight a star hits Rot; Dawn: **Star storm**, stuns every Blight a star hits, and lightning jumps on to 1 more.

## The Attunement

Once a run, the Wardlight takes an element. You choose it when the Wardlight reaches IV, no sooner than day 10 (the day before night 10), and only while a mage tower's keystone stands in the village (Sunfall, Earthwound, Throne of Storms, Thunderstone, Crown of Frost, Sigil of Ruin, Wicker Giant and Hive of Light). From then on every tower, every spell and the rallied villagers carry the element's **tint**, and arrows and stones go through 40 % of the Blight's armour. Attuning never raises the night's Blight. The village and the world take the element's colours.

|  | Ember | Verdant | Dawn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promise | Watch the world burn | Let the rot spread | Smite everything |
| Tint on every hit | Leaves a burning patch on the ground, 2.25 a second for 4 s | Rot: 1 poison stack for 6 s; every 2 s each Poisoned Blight passes it to the nearest unpoisoned one within 3 m | Chains a smite (6) to 1 more Blight within 4 m, Stun 0.25 s, and lights the Mist where it lands |
| Signature spell | Rain of Fire: brings fire down on a whole side of the village | Plague Wind: carries the Rot you have sown through the whole crowd | Judgement: strikes down every Blight in your light at once |
| Available | Free, from the start | Needs the full game | Needs the full game |

### Ember

- **Spreading:** Every burning Blight sets one Blight within 2 m alight each second, oiled or not.
- **Fire towers:** Fireball, Pyre and Brimstone towers set off Ignite on every hit.

### Verdant

- **Poison:** A Blight holds up to 15 poison stacks, 20 from Venom, and passes them to the 5 nearest when it dies.
- **Hex towers:** Hex and curse towers set off Poison spread on every hit.

### Dawn

- **Lightning:** Every lightning chain jumps 2 more times, from towers and from the Wardlight alike.
- **Storm towers:** Lightning, chain and storm coil towers set off Conduct on every hit.

### Signature spells

Each Attunement brings its signature spell, cast once a night for free. The Memory sells a second signature for each element (100 Echoes each, with the full game), taken instead of the first.

#### Rain of Fire (Ember)

Brings fire down on a whole side of the village.

- **Cast:** Sets every Blight on the side you tap Burning, from the Wardlight out into the Mist.

Numbers: 2.5 a second for 8 s, a 60° side.

- **Tip:** Save it for the side the biggest surge comes from.

#### Plague Wind (Verdant)

Carries the Rot you have sown through the whole crowd.

- **Cast:** Doubles the Rot on every rotting Blight.
- **Spread:** Gives Rot to every Blight next to a rotting one.

Numbers: 5 m.

- **Tip:** Cast it when most of a surge is already Rotting.

#### Judgement (Dawn)

Strikes down every Blight in your light at once.

- **Cast:** Hits and Stuns every Blight standing in your light, wherever you tap.

Numbers: 20 damage, Stunned 2 s.

- **Tip:** Wait until a surge is inside your light.

#### Phoenix (Ember) — replaces Rain of Fire

Sets the Wardlight ablaze and raises your fallen buildings.

- **Cast:** Sets every Blight round the Wardlight Burning.
- **Rebuild:** Stands every fallen building up again at full health.

Numbers: 2.5 a second for 4 s, 10 m.

- **Tip:** Keep it for the night your walls fall.

#### Thornbloom (Verdant) — replaces Plague Wind

Grows thorns through all of your light.

- **Cast:** Slows every Blight in your light and gives it Rot.

Numbers: 20 s.

- **Tip:** Cast it early in a surge, so the thorns work on the whole crowd.

#### Eclipse (Dawn) — replaces Judgement

Darkens the sky over your light and stops the Blight in it.

- **Cast:** Hits and Stuns every Blight standing in your light.

Numbers: 15 damage, Stunned 3 s.

- **Tip:** Cast it when a surge is at your walls, while your towers can reach it.

## Dares

Every day, the first included, Tonight offers three Dares: a harder night for Knowledge and supplies, paid at the next dawn.

| Dare | Does | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Swarm | Brings 30 % more Blight tonight. | 10 Knowledge, 8 supplies |
| Brutes | Sends 2 more Brutes tonight. | 10 Knowledge, 10 supplies |
| Hungry wards | Doubles the mana your wards draw tonight. | 10 Knowledge, 6 supplies |

The Brutes Dare is only offered in a run whose Blight includes Brutes.
