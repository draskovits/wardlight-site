Many buildings leave the Blight in a **state**. Four of them are combo states: one building **sets** the state and another **uses** it, setting off an event on the field (a synergy). Using a state spends it (poison spreads instead).

## The states

| State | What it does |
|---|---|
| Oiled | Ready for fire: any fire may set off Ignite on it |
| Wet | Ready for lightning and ice: a lightning hit may Conduct through it, and cold counts twice (Flash freeze). Spike pits leave it 6 s |
| Chilled | 30 % slower. Stacks from every cold source add up; 4 stacks freeze the Blight. A stack lasts 4 s |
| Frozen | Stands still for 2.5 s. Ready for stones and bolts to Shatter |
| Frost-hardened | A Blight Frozen 2 times within 15 s takes no Chilled stacks and can't be Frozen for 8 s |
| Poisoned | Stacks: each takes 0.6 % of the Blight's full health a second through armour, up to 10 stacks (16 with Venom); they last 4 s after the last one and fall off together. Each stack also slows it 3 %, up to 45 % |
| Burning | Damage over time that ignores armour. Rain halves how long it lasts; summer's heat makes it last 50 % longer |
| Slowed | 40 % slower; only one slow counts |
| Stunned | Stands still and doesn't attack, each source for its own time. Knock-backs push and Stun briefly |
| Marked | Takes 25 % more damage |
| Revealed | Seen in the Mist and in fog; a Shade is seen at all only while Revealed (or in bright light) |
| Lit | In Sheltered light the Blight walks at 60 % of its speed |
| Diving | A Wisp over its last 4 m to a light: stones and fire reach it then |

**Bosses resist the cold:** a boss needs 2× the Chilled stacks, stays Frozen 50 % as long and is Frost-hardened after its first freeze. Bosses take 25 % of every synergy event's damage and of each poison stack.

## The synergies

**The chance:** a using building sets off its event with its own chance: a tree's base tower about 35 %, a specialisation 60 %, a keystone 100 %; a fire with no building of its own (a talent's or a spell's) 35 %. Chance talents add 15 points for their tree's towers. One roll per hit; a miss is a plain hit and the state stays. Setting a state never rolls. **Overkill feeds the event:** the damage a using hit wastes on its victim grows the event by up to 100 % (its reach, its line, its spread). An event never sets off another event.

### Ignite (Oiled + fire)

The oil burns off in a flare: 30 fire damage through armour, jumping from Oiled Blight to Oiled Blight within the Inferno's reach (2 m), at most 12 jumps. Each catches the Inferno's ×3 burn, and a trench under any of them goes up.

**Sets Oiled:** oil trenches and Black Spring, the Oil flood spell, the bog field's Tarred talent, the watch lantern's Tripwire flare, the Lamplighters' Hall's Oil jars, the stone wall's Murder holes, the smokery's Tar smoke. **Uses it:** every fire: fireball, Pyre and Brimstone towers, Sunfall, Earthwound, the fire-pot mangonel, Dragon's Mouth, the lanterns' bursts, Fire arrows, the burning glass and the Eye of the Sun.

### Conduct and Flash freeze (Wet + lightning or cold)

**Conduct:** a lightning hit on a Wet Blight runs as a net through every Wet Blight joined to it (at most 3 m between two, up to 16), dealing the hit's damage to each, and the net dries. A chain tower's jump through a Wet Blight is free. **Flash freeze:** every cold source adds 2 Chilled stacks to a Wet Blight instead of 1, with no roll.

**Sets Wet:** spike pits (6 s), bog fields (twice as long with Iron Thicket), the netcasters' nets, the field's Ditches, the timber wall's Green timber, the Throne of Storms' rain, rain weather (every Blight) and the Storm season boon. **Uses it:** the lightning tower, chain tower, storm coil, Thunderstone and Throne of Storms (Conduct); the ice tower (Flash freeze).

### Shatter (Frozen + stones or bolts)

A stone or bolt that hits a Frozen Blight may break it for 4× the hit as true damage (through armour). A stone's shards fly on in a 5 m cone (60°) for 75 % of that; a ballista's bolt flies on 12 m through the frozen line.

**The ice tower's call:** each Blight an ice tower freezes calls the nearest mangonel or ballista in reach to shoot it at once, once every 4 s. **Sets Frozen:** the ice tower (its 4th hit, 2nd on a Wet Blight; Cold snap one sooner), the Crown of Frost, the Hoarfrost spell, the bog field's Frostmoor, the split prism's Cold light, the ice tower's Cold iron. **Uses it:** the mangonel, stone thrower and The Long Arm; the silver ballista, Falcon's watch and The Last Witness.

### Poison spread (Poisoned + volleys and helpers)

**Spread:** half the hit Blight's stacks (at least 1, not spent) are handed out one at a time round the 6 nearest Blight within 3 m. **When slain:** a Blight that dies Poisoned passes 50 % of its stacks to the 3 nearest within 4.5 m, never rolled.

**Sets Poisoned:** hex towers (3 stacks a hit, 5 with Venom), curse towers and their fields, the Sigil of Ruin, Spell mark, the Bane flare, the stone wall's Quicklime, the cider press's Sour vats, Verdant's Rot. **Uses it:** volley and repeater towers, the Keep of Iron Rain and Fletchers' Hall; the summoning circle's helpers (hounds, golems, glimmers, the Hive of Light).

## Named combos

The dawn report credits these: Lantern snare (a lantern's lure, spike pits and its burst), Inferno (a burning trench), Ignite, Conduct, Flash freeze, Shatter, Poison spread and Searchlight (a kill only because the Blight was Revealed).

## Weather against you

- Rain halves how long Burning lasts (×0.5) and stops trenches, but leaves every Blight Wet.
- Wind carries fire further downwind and shortens arrows upwind.
- Sealing a ward blinds the towers in its light.
