Light is where you build and what your towers can see. Wards light the village and mend its walls with mana; lanterns lure; prisms and flares push the sight line into the Mist.

**How to read the numbers.** Costs are the spring price in supplies. Every building and every growth step costs ×1 in spring, ×1.2 in summer, ×1.5 in autumn and ×2 in winter, and 5 % more for every copy of it already standing (walls, trenches and caltrops never count). A build cell is 2 m. A building without its own health number has 150. Talents cost Knowledge only, never supplies. On a building's card, **Level 2** costs 50 % of the building's price and makes it do 25 % more; growing it into a specialisation or keystone costs the difference to that building's price, at least 50 % of its own. Damage a second is worked out from the damage and the rate, into one target before armour.

In this page: Ward, Lantern, Prism, Flare post.

## Ward

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Spreads the light you build in and your towers see by.

- **Light:** Grows 0.5 m each dawn it was fed with mana, up to 4 m more.
- **Mend:** Mends the walls in its light with mana at night.
- **Reveal:** Reveals the Blight up to 2 m past its light.

- **Strong against:** Shades: it Reveals them 2 m past its light, and they take double from it.
- **Weak against:** Manaleeches and Wisps: both go for your light, and a Wisp's burst takes 12 mana.
- **Tip:** Seal a ward you don't need tonight, and it draws no mana.
- **Tip:** The Blight is drawn to light. Your light decides which side they come from.
- **Advisor's tip:** A ward only grows on the dawns it was fed. Seal the ones facing nothing tonight and feed the ones facing the Blight.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | 10 supplies (spring price) |
| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Health | 150 |
| Light | 9 m |
| Mana upkeep | Draws 0.2 mana a second all night (at 9 m) |

**Base talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Bright crystal** (10 Knowledge): Adds +2 m light, so you build, see and mend further out. *Bounty:* Makes tonight's bounty pay 10 % more.
- **Banked light** (10 Knowledge): Makes a ward cost no mana at night while no Blight is in its light.
- **Reserve** (10 Knowledge): Keeps a ward at full light 10 s after its mana runs out, once a night.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built ward at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Mender ward (specialisation)

A ward that mends walls and buildings faster.

- **Mend:** Mends twice as fast, and 3 m past its light.

- **Strong against:** Spitters and Crawlers: it mends their small hits on your walls twice as fast.
- **Weak against:** Manaleeches and Wisps: they go for your wards first.
- **Tip:** Put it behind the wall the Blight hits hardest.
- **Advisor's tip:** It mends twice as fast as a plain ward. Put it behind the wall that takes the worst, and leave the light to the others.

## Lantern

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Lures Crawlers to it and bursts into fire when broken.

- **Lure:** Draws the Crawlers within 12 m toward it.
- **When broken:** Bursts and sets everything within 4 m Burning 4 s.
- **At dusk:** Lights again for free every night.

- **Strong against:** Crawlers: it lures them from 12 m and bursts into fire when they break it.
- **Weak against:** Everything but Crawlers: its lure draws only Crawlers.
- **Tip:** Dig spike pits beside it for a Lantern snare.
- **Advisor's tip:** Crawlers can't resist a light, a weakness I share. Dig spike pits beside it and they walk into them on the way.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | 3 supplies (spring price) |
| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Health | 40 |
| Light | 3 m |
| Lure | Draws Crawlers within 12 m |
| Burst | When broken, sets everything within 4 m Burning 4 s at 5 a second |
| Synergy | Ignite on Oiled Blight, 35 % chance a hit ([synergies](/guide/states-and-synergies/)) |

**Base talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Fish oil** (10 Knowledge): Lures Crawlers from 14 m.
- **Twin wick** (10 Knowledge): Keeps a lantern standing and lit after its first burst, once a night.
- **Lure wick** (10 Knowledge): Makes every lantern lure from 6 m further.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built lantern at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.

### Lure lantern (specialisation)

A lantern that lures every Blight on foot but Brutes.

- **Strong against:** Crawlers and Spitters: it draws everything on foot within 16 m.
- **Weak against:** Brutes and Wisps: it never draws a Brute, and Wisps dive at it.
- **Tip:** Dig spike pits beside it for a Lantern snare.
- **Advisor's tip:** It draws everything on foot within 16 m except Brutes. Dig spike pits round it and set it where your towers can see.

## Prism

**Learn it** for 10 Knowledge to put it on the build bar. It waits for unlock tier 3 (see [unlock tiers](/guide/memory-and-difficulty/)).

Casts a ward's light as a cone into the Mist.

- **Light:** Catches a ward within 6 m and throws its light 24 m.
- **Turn:** Turns any time, at night too.
- **Mana:** Draws no mana of its own.

- **Strong against:** Shades and Spitters in the Mist: its cone lights 24 m out.
- **Weak against:** Manaleeches: they go for the ward it takes its light from.
- **Tip:** Point it at the side tonight's Blight comes from.
- **Advisor's tip:** A prism has no light of its own, it borrows a ward's within 6 m. Turn it toward tonight's Blight before the night starts.

| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Unlocks | At unlock tier 3 (once 3 lands are open, see [unlock tiers](/guide/memory-and-difficulty/)) |

## Flare post

**Learn it** for 10 Knowledge to put it on the build bar.

Lights up any spot at night with a flare.

- **Flare:** Lights 8 m for 15 s.
- **At night:** Fires 2 a night, wherever you tap within 40 m.

- **Strong against:** Spitters and crowds out of sight: a flare lights 8 m for 15 s where you tap.
- **Weak against:** Late surges: it has only 2 flares a night.
- **Tip:** Fire it where your towers can't see the crowd.
- **Advisor's tip:** You get 2 flares a night, so don't waste them on the first Crawler. Fire them where your towers can't see the crowd.

### Bane flare (specialisation)

Fires a sick green flare that poisons the Blight in its light.

- **Flare:** Lights 8 m for 15 s in a sick green.
- **Light:** Puts a poison stack on every Blight in its light, and one more every 4 s it stays.
- **At night:** Fires 2 a night, wherever you tap within 40 m.

- **Strong against:** Brutes and crowds held at your wall: its green light leaves every Blight in it Poisoned, armour or not.
- **Weak against:** Blight that runs through: it gathers few stacks, and they wear off 4 s after it leaves the light.
- **Tip:** Fire it on the crowd held at your wall, in reach of a volley tower.
- **Advisor's tip:** That's lamp oil gone bad. I meant to pour it away. It burns green and every Blight in its light is Poisoned, so fire it on the Brute at your wall.
