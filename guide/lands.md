A run is played on one land. The lands open in order: Witchwood → Stonehold → Fenlands → Rosegarth → Highlands → Ashwood. The first is open from the start; each later one opens with a win on the one before it **and** the full game. Every land has 12 nights in four seasons but moves nights between the seasons, has a building no other land has, favourite kinds of Blight that come as its own variants, and its own final boss on night 12.

## 1. Witchwood

The first land, open from the start.

- **Seasons:** Spring 3 (nights 1–3), Summer 3 (nights 4–6), Autumn 3 (nights 7–9), Winter 3 (nights 10–12). The harvest dawn follows night 9.
- **Own building:** the hunting lodge (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | clear 60 %, wind 40 % |
| Autumn | clear 20 %, wind 45 %, rain 10 %, fog 25 % |
| Winter | clear 40 %, hard frost 40 %, thaw 20 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- Forest everywhere: woodcutters clear the ground. A lake of deep water: the walking Blight goes round it, Burrowers dig under and flyers fly over; it freezes in winter.

**Favourite Blight:** Wisp and Shade. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

### Crown Wisp (its Wisp)

Hides in unlit treetops, out of sight and reach.

- **Dive:** Dives from 12 m out, not 4.

- **Tip:** Build on the forest edge or light it.

### Wood Shade (its Shade)

Hides under the trees, where a Reveal soon fades.

- **Under the trees:** Stays Revealed for 2 s at most.

- **Tip:** Build on the forest edge or light it.

**Final boss (night 12):** Mother Below. Tunnels under your village and breaks buildings where she rises. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).

## 2. Stonehold

Stands in the angle of two cliffs, so everything comes at one wall.

- **Opens:** a win on the Witchwood and the full game.
- **Seasons:** Spring 3 (nights 1–3), Summer 3 (nights 4–6), Autumn 3 (nights 7–9), Winter 3 (nights 10–12). The harvest dawn follows night 9.
- **Own building:** the silver mine (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).
- **Sides:** only 2 neighbouring sides of the 6 are ever open (the gorge between the cliffs), so the whole night comes at one front.
- **Building ground:** buildings stand only on the marked home ground (walls also on the old wall's footing, traps wherever the light allows, the land's own building on its shafts).
- **Rock outcrops:** none.

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | clear 60 %, wind 40 % |
| Autumn | clear 20 %, wind 45 %, rain 10 %, fog 25 % |
| Winter | clear 40 %, hard frost 40 %, thaw 20 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- Cliffs: block the Blight.
- **Old shafts:** 8, where the silver mine stands. The oldest one standing is where the final boss comes up.
- **Ruined wards:** 1 of 3 spots; a ward built on one costs no supplies.
- **Caches:** 2 of 4 spots; the first time your light reaches one it pays 10–20 supplies.

**Favourite Blight:** Brute and Burrower. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

**Final boss (night 12):** The Glass Bearer. Comes up your oldest shaft, behind every tower, bearing a glass no shot can pass. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).

## 3. Fenlands

Lives off the ponds with the fisher's hut, and the Blight comes through the reeds.

- **Opens:** a win on Stonehold and the full game.
- **Seasons:** Spring 3 (nights 1–3), Summer 3 (nights 4–6), Autumn 4 (nights 7–10), Winter 2 (nights 11–12). The harvest dawn follows night 10.
- **Own building:** the fisher's hut (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).
- **Blight:** ×1.1 the usual nights' budget.
- **Ice:** the water freezes at night 10's dusk, opening the water as a side.
- **Spring flood:** dry ground within 2 m of water takes nothing until summer, and the fords take no walls or traps.
- **Lighthouse:** a ward on the high ground by the bay lights ×1.5 as far.
- **Rock outcrops:** 2.

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | clear 60 %, wind 40 % |
| Autumn | rain 40 %, fog 45 %, clear 15 % |
| Winter | hard frost 100 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- Deep water: the Blight can't cross it (Burrowers go under, flyers over) until winter freezes it.
- Reeds: shallow water; the Blight wades slowly, and towers see it there only in Sheltered light.
- Soil.
- High ground.
- **Fords:** 3 of 5 fords across the water are open each run: shallow lanes the Blight walks in by.
- **Reed channel:** 1 of 3 is drawn each run: a shallow way in through the reeds.
- **Ruined wards:** 1 of 3 spots; a ward built on one costs no supplies.
- **Caches:** 2 of 4 spots; the first time your light reaches one it pays 10–20 supplies.

**Favourite Blight:** Bloater and Burrower. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

### Marsh-gas Bloater (its Bloater)

Drifts over the water straight at the Wardlight.

- **On contact:** Bursts on the first building it touches.
- **Cloud:** Leaves a cloud for 4 s that hurts rallied villagers in it.

- **Tip:** Keep the Rally out of its cloud.

### Mud Burrower (its Burrower)

Digs under the fords and comes up on your island's shore.

- **From night 10:** Walks over the ice instead of digging.

- **Tip:** Keep towers along your shore.

**Final boss (night 12):** Drowned King. Wades the deep mere where no ford runs, and puts out every fire near it. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).

## 4. Rosegarth

A garden of yew hedges. The Blight walks the paths, and you choose which gates to shut.

- **Opens:** a win on the Fenlands and the full game.
- **Seasons:** Spring 3 (nights 1–3), Summer 3 (nights 4–6), Autumn 3 (nights 7–9), Winter 3 (nights 10–12). The harvest dawn follows night 9.
- **Own building:** the orchard (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).
- **The Wardlight's light:** starts at 14 m (usually 11 m).
- **Rock outcrops:** none.

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | clear 60 %, wind 40 % |
| Autumn | clear 20 %, wind 45 %, rain 10 %, fog 25 % |
| Winter | clear 40 %, hard frost 40 %, thaw 20 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- Hedges: clipped yew. The Blight can't pass a hedge and nothing is built on one.
- Deep water: the Blight can't cross it (Burrowers go under, flyers over) until winter freezes it.
- **Gates:** Brow Gate, Nose Gate, Lip Gate, Chin Gate, Nape Gate and Crown Gate, one a side; each takes a wall and nothing else, and walling one shut turns the Blight to the next way round.
- **Ruined wards:** 1 spot; a ward built on one costs no supplies.
- **Caches:** 2 of 4 spots; the first time your light reaches one it pays 10–20 supplies.

**Favourite Blight:** Shade and Spitter. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

**Final boss (night 12):** The Thornless. Cuts his own way through your hedges, and the gate you kept open grows shut behind him. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).

## 5. Highlands

Gives you rock all round and long views, and little wood.

- **Opens:** a win on Rosegarth and the full game.
- **Seasons:** Spring 3 (nights 1–3), Summer 3 (nights 4–6), Autumn 2 (nights 7–8), Winter 4 (nights 9–12). The harvest dawn follows night 8.
- **Own building:** the quarry (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).
- **Blight:** ×0.9 the usual nights' budget.
- **Rock outcrops:** 9.

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | wind 60 %, clear 40 % |
| Autumn | clear 20 %, wind 45 %, rain 10 %, fog 25 % |
| Winter | hard frost 50 %, clear 30 %, thaw 20 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- High ground.
- Cliffs: block the Blight.
- Deep water: the Blight can't cross it (Burrowers go under, flyers over) until winter freezes it.
- **Meadow terraces:** 3 of 6 are drawn each run: open ground to build on.
- **Passes:** 4 of 5 passes through the ridge are open each run, one front per pass.
- **Scree:** 1 of 3 is drawn each run: a cliff until night 9's dusk, then a way over the ridge.
- **Tarn:** a small lake of deep water in the valley, 1 of 4 spots.
- **Ruined wards:** 2 of 5 spots; a ward built on one costs no supplies.
- **Caches:** 2 of 4 spots; the first time your light reaches one it pays 10–20 supplies.

**Favourite Blight:** Brute and Spitter. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

### Rock-breaker (its Brute)

Hits wooden walls twice as hard as a Brute.

- **Blow:** Breaks a palisade in 4 s.

- **Tip:** Shut the passes with stone walls.

### Cliff Spitter (its Spitter)

Spits from 3 m further back than other Spitters.

- **Tip:** Build your watchtowers right against the wall.

**Final boss (night 12):** Mountain's Fist. Climbs the ridge where no pass runs and tears a way through it. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).

## 6. Ashwood

Puts you in a burnt forest that is dark from the first night. Ember feels at home here.

- **Opens:** a win on the Highlands and the full game.
- **Seasons:** Spring 2 (nights 1–2), Summer 5 (nights 3–7), Autumn 3 (nights 8–10), Winter 2 (nights 11–12). The harvest dawn follows night 10.
- **Own building:** the field (see [village buildings](/guide/village-buildings/)).
- **Blight:** ×1.2 the usual nights' budget.
- **The Wardlight's light:** starts at 7 m (usually 11 m).
- **Sides:** 1 from night 1; 2 from night 3; 3 from night 5; 6 from night 12.
- **Dry summer:** in a clear summer, fields more than 6 m from water pay ×0.875 from the summer's second dawn.
- **Rock outcrops:** 2.

**Weather chances:**

| Season | Weather |
|---|---|
| Spring | clear 40 %, rain 40 %, thaw 20 % |
| Summer | clear 50 %, wind 50 % |
| Autumn | clear 20 %, wind 45 %, rain 10 %, fog 25 % |
| Winter | clear 40 %, hard frost 40 %, thaw 20 % |

**Terrain and places:**

- Ash: the burnt forest floor.
- **Green grove:** 1 of 5 spots.
- **Ember pits:** 4 of 10 spots; they glow at night, and a Crawler that walks within 8 m of one turns to it and stands in its glow 3 s, once a night.
- **Ruined wards:** 1 of 3 spots; a ward built on one costs no supplies.
- **Caches:** 2 of 4 spots; the first time your light reaches one it pays 10–20 supplies.

**Favourite Blight:** Crawler and Wisp. A favourite is ×2 as likely in the run's draw (Crawlers come in every run), comes as the land's own variant below, and has ×1.5 health on night 10.

### Ash Crawler (its Crawler)

Twice as many come, each with half a Crawler's health.

- **Weakness:** Takes twice the damage from fire and stones.
- **At a pit:** Turns to an ember pit within 8 m and stands in its glow for 3 s before it marches on.

- **Tip:** Meet the packs with a mangonel.

### Spark Wisp (its Wisp)

Takes the ember pits for lights.

- **At a pit:** Dives on an ember pit nearer than any of your lights and bursts there without harm.

- **Tip:** Build your lights behind the pits.

**Final boss (night 12):** Cinder Stag. Bounds through your village, burning, and sets alight all it passes. See [the Blight](/guide/the-blight/).
