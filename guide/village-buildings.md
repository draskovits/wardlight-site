Village buildings house your villagers, pay supplies at dawn, bring Knowledge and make mana at night. Each land also has one building of its own (marked below).

**How to read the numbers.** Costs are the spring price in supplies. Every building and every growth step costs ×1 in spring, ×1.2 in summer, ×1.5 in autumn and ×2 in winter, and 5 % more for every copy of it already standing (walls, trenches and caltrops never count). A build cell is 2 m. A building without its own health number has 150. Talents cost Knowledge only, never supplies. On a building's card, **Level 2** costs 50 % of the building's price and makes it do 25 % more; growing it into a specialisation or keystone costs the difference to that building's price, at least 50 % of its own. Damage a second is worked out from the damage and the rate, into one target before armour.

In this page: House, Woodcutter, Library, Hunting lodge, Quarry, Shrine, Field, Fisher's hut, Silver mine, Orchard.

## House

**In the starting kit:** on the build bar from the first day, its tree open.

Homes villagers, and every workplace needs them to pay.

- **Villagers:** Homes 4 villagers.
- **Blessed:** Makes mana at night when a shrine blesses it. With more than 4 villagers it prays harder, a share more for each.

- **Weak against:** Burrowers: they dig under your walls and come up at houses.
- **Tip:** Build houses in small clusters.
- **Advisor's tip:** They pack 4 to a roof and still complain. A shrine or a library in the middle of a cluster has houses to count.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Cost | 5 supplies (spring price) |
| Size | 1 × 1 build cells (2 × 2 m) |
| Villagers | Homes 4 |
| Neighbours | +1 villager for each other house within 6 m, up to +2, only with the talent Crowded hearths |
| Health | 150 |

**Base talents** (buy at most 2 of the 3):

- **Crowded hearths** (10 Knowledge): Gives every house +1 villager for each house within 6 m, up to 2.
- **Stone floors** (10 Knowledge): Gives houses +50 % health. *Burrowers:* Can't dig into a house and come up 4 m away instead, Revealed.
- **Lamps** (10 Knowledge): Gives each house 4 m of light to build in and for towers to see by.

**Specialisations:** once 2 of the base talents are bought and the Wardlight is at II, learn a specialisation (20 Knowledge, the second 40). A built house at Level 2 then grows into it in place on its card.
