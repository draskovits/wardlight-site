The Wardlight is the same spirit in every run, and it remembers. Every run, lost or won, earns **Echoes**, spent in **the Memory** on small permanent bonuses, heirlooms and new options. A first run may be lost; the Memory makes the next one stronger.

## Echoes

- 5 Echoes for every night held, and 30 more for a win, × the Darkness' Echo bonus (and the random run's).
- **Feats** pay Echoes once each (below).

## The full game

Wardlight is free to download and play. One in-app purchase, the **full game**, adds: the lands after the Witchwood (each still opens with a win on the land before it); the Verdant and Dawn Attunements and every second signature spell (Ember stays free, so a free run can be won); and the Memory after the first 3 runs (without it the Memory can be spent in for 3 runs; then it rests: what is lit keeps working and Echoes keep coming, to spend once it is bought). Mutators and the random run stay free.

## The Memory's sky

The Memory is one night sky of constellations. Its stars are of two kinds: **strength stars** (small nodes and heirlooms) and **options stars** (things runs can offer). A star opens once the star before it is lit.

- **Small nodes** give one small permanent bonus each and always work once lit. Nodes on the same bonus add up. Every node costs 8 + 0.75 × the nodes already bought in the whole sky, rounded (the first 8).
- **Heirlooms** hang off the paths. Each is bought once and **carried in a slot** of its kind: 4 Start slots for **Hearths** (a better start), 2 Perk slots for **Oaths** (rule-changers for the whole run), 1 Attunement slot for a **Flame** (works once the Wardlight attunes). Hearths cost 60 Echoes unless noted; some are free. The loadout stays between runs.
- **The lit sky's weight:** the more of the strength sky is lit, the brighter the village (the Wardlight's and wards' light × (1 + 0.05 × the share lit)) and the more Blight each night brings (× (1 + 0.25 × the share lit²)): more Blight, never tougher.

A new Memory carries The old wall, Old watchtower, Settlers, Kept stores and Steady flame.

## Unlock tiers

Your **unlock tier** is 1 plus every land past the first that is open. Tier 2 (the second land open) adds the magic-proof Blight (Hexward, Manaleech, Husk Knight) and the silver ballista's tree; tier 3 adds the Spitter and the prism's tree. Once unlocked, they come on every land.

## The Darkness

The Darkness is the difficulty ladder, chosen before each run. A new profile starts at Clear night; Waning Moon opens after the first finished run; each next level opens by winning the one below. Each level changes the Blight's health, the night's budget and what the workplaces pay, and from Half Moon on brings **hardships** (mutators drawn for the run, shown in the Almanac). Half Moon is the year as tuned.

**Waning Moon:** A mild year. The Blight is a little weaker and fewer than at Half Moon, and the workplaces pay a tenth more.

**Half Moon:** The year at full strength. The Blight as it is, the workplaces at their plain pay. One hardship falls on the year.

**Crescent:** Makes the Blight tougher and the nights bigger, and the workplaces pay a little less. Brutes come from night 4. Two hardships fall on the year.

**New Moon:** Makes the Blight much tougher and the nights bigger, and the workplaces pay a tenth less. Burrowers come from night 8. Three hardships fall on the year.

**Eclipse:** Makes every Blight far tougher and the nights far bigger, and the workplaces pay less. Brutes come from night 3, Burrowers from night 7. Four hardships fall on the year.

## Mutators (hardships)

Mutators make a run harder. From Half Moon on, each Darkness level draws that many for the run (Brutes Early is left out where the level already brings Brutes as early); a random run draws its own on top.

| Mutator | Does | Echoes (random run) |
|---|---|---|
| Thick Mist | The Mist starts 2 m closer to every light. | +15 % |
| Restless Dead | Hold a night well and the next one is stronger. Struggle and it is weaker. | +25 % |
| Lean Years | Every workplace pays 1 supply less. | +20 % |
| Quick Blight | The Blight moves 20 % faster. | +20 % |
| Brutes Early | Brutes come from night 4. | +30 % |
| Dry Wells | Mana flows in 25 % slower at night. | +20 % |

**Restless Dead** in numbers: a clean night (the Wardlight losing under 50 health and at most 1 building lost) raises the next night's budget by 10 %; a hurt one (300 damage or 4 buildings lost) lowers it; between ×0.9 and ×1.5.

## The random run

Opens after the first finished run. The game draws the land, the heirlooms, the spells, the Attunement and 1–2 mutators for you; it pays Echoes ×1.25 and milestones: night 4: 5 Echoes; night 8: 10 Echoes and a free options star; night 12: 20 Echoes and a free options star.

## Score

A run's score: 1,000 for each night held, 50 for each building standing at the end (not walls, ruins or the Wardlight), up to 3,000 for the Wardlight's health at the end (0 when it fell), and 10 for each Blight in the last night's best combo, all × the Darkness' score bonus. The best score goes to the Game Center leaderboard.
