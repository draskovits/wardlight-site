Support

Help and contact

Found a bug, have an idea or a question about the game? Write to hello@playwardlight.com or open an issue on GitHub, and I'll answer as soon as I can.

For a bug, it helps to say which device you play on, which land and night you were on, and what happened.

Questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Wardlight is a single-player game. It plays fully offline and has no account.

Is my run saved?

Yes. The game saves at every dawn, on your device. If you close it during a night, you continue from that night's dawn.

How long is a run?

About forty minutes: twelve nights, each with an untimed day before it. You can stop after any dawn.

Is Wardlight free?

The download is free, and so is every run with the Ember Attunement. One in-app purchase, Full Game, adds the Verdant and Dawn Attunements and keeps the Memory open after your third run. Purchases go through the App Store.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the ••• menu, then Full game, then Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I turn off the play data?

Yes: Settings → Apps → Wardlight → Share play data. What is sent is in the privacy policy.
