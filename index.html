Questions

Asked so far

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. Wardlight is a single-player game. It plays fully offline and has no account.

How long is a run?

Twelve nights, each with an untimed day before it: about forty minutes. The game saves at every dawn, so a run fits an evening or a few train rides.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. It isn't on Android, PC or consoles.

Is my run saved?

Yes, at every dawn, on your device. If you close the game during a night, you continue from that night's dawn.

What does the Full Game add?

The Verdant and Dawn Attunements, and the Memory, the upgrades between runs, stays open after your third run. It is shared through Family Sharing. Prices differ a little by country.

Does the game collect data?

Only anonymous play data: which lands are played, on which night runs end, which talents are picked. Nothing that tells me who you are. You can turn it off in Settings → Apps → Wardlight → Share play data. Details are in the privacy policy.

I bought the full game on another device.

Purchases go through the App Store. Open the ••• menu, then Full game, then Restore purchase, with the same Apple Account.

I found a bug, or I have an idea.

Write to hello@playwardlight.com or open an issue on GitHub. It helps to say which device you play on, which land and night you were on, and what happened.
