Contact & help

Found a bug, have an idea or a question about the game? Open an issue on GitHub and I'll answer as soon as I can.

For a bug, it helps to say which device you play on, which night you were on and what happened.

Questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Wardlight is a single-player game. It plays fully offline and has no account.

Is my run saved?

Yes. The game saves at every dawn, on your device. If you close it during a night, you continue from that night's dawn.

How long is a run?

About forty minutes: twelve nights, each with an untimed day before it. You can stop after any dawn.

Is Wardlight free?

The download is free. The full game is unlocked with an in-app purchase. Purchases go through the App Store and can be restored on any device with the same Apple Account.
