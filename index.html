Wardlight is a city builder and tower defence for iPhone and iPad. By day you build inside your light. By night the Blight crawl out of the Mist, thousands of them, toward whatever burns brightest.

I · The rule

Light is land.

You are the Wardlight, the flame at the heart of a village on the edge of an endless Mist. You can only build where your light reaches, and your towers only see lit ground.

A bigger light means a bigger village. A brighter village draws more of the Blight. Where you grow decides where they come.

II · The year

The day waits for you.

Spend the village's supplies on houses, fields, walls and towers. Read where tonight's Blight will come from. Then call the night when you are ready, not a second before.

The night does not.

The Blight come in surges. Your towers fight what the light shows them, and your light makes mana, which you spend on spells and mage towers wherever the wall is about to break.

2,500Blight alive at once

Each one is simulated on your phone, twenty times a second, and drawn by a renderer written for this game in Apple's Metal. Nothing runs on a server. Every crawler you see is walking toward your wall.

III · Growth

Every building has its own tree.

Knowledge from the night buys talents on each building's tree. A house grows into a longhouse, a watchtower into a volley tower, and every tree ends in a keystone that changes how the building plays.

Each run is a new year with new spells to choose, and two trees kindled at half the price.

Ember

Every hit leaves fire behind it. Free for everyone.

Verdant

Rot spreads from one Blight to the next, through the whole crowd.

Dawn

Lightning leaps from one to the next and stuns where it lands.

At its fourth level the Wardlight takes an element. Every tower, spell and light in the village takes its colour for the rest of the year.

IV · The lands

Six lands, one after another.

Each land has its own ground, its own Blight and its own boss on the twelfth night. You open the next by holding the one before. When a land gets easy, raise the Darkness: every level adds a hardship and pays more Echoes.

Witchwood

The old wood and its lake. The water keeps the walkers out until winter turns it to ice.

Night 12: the Mother Below

Fenlands

An island of fen soil in a deep mere, reached by a few fords. The water guards you until it freezes.

Night 12: the Drowned King

Highlands

A shelf of high ground ringed by cliffs. You see them coming through the passes, and you feed the village from stone.

Night 12: the Mountain's Fist

Ashwood

A burnt forest, open on every side and dark from the first night. Ember pits glow like lanterns, and fire is your friend.

Night 12: the Cinder Stag

Stonehold

A court cut into the angle of two cliffs, the rock at your back. Everything comes at one wall, and ground is scarce.

Night 12: the Glass Bearer

Rosegarth

Her garden. The Blight cannot pass a hedge, so the hedges choose the roads and you choose the gates. Seen from above, they draw her face.

V · The story

A dwarf who wants to retire, and an elf who wants the dark.

The lampwright

An old dwarf who carries your ember from village to village and would much rather be fishing. Forty years of her war, thirty of her halls, and still not one fish.

Lady Vaelith

She spreads the Mist so that no light can ever fall on the tiny scar on her cheek. She has something to say about every night you survive.

VII · Questions

Asked so far

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. Wardlight is a single-player game. It plays fully offline and has no account.

How long is a run?

Twelve nights, each with an untimed day before it: about forty minutes. The game saves at every dawn, so a run fits an evening or a few train rides.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. It isn't on Android, PC or consoles.

Is my run saved?

Yes, at every dawn, on your device. If you close the game during a night, you continue from that night's dawn.

Does the game collect data?

Only anonymous play data: which lands are played, on which night runs end, which talents are picked. Nothing that tells me who you are. You can turn it off in Settings → Apps → Wardlight → Share play data. Details are in the privacy policy.

I bought the full game on another device.

Purchases go through the App Store. Open the ••• menu, then Full game, then Restore purchase, with the same Apple Account.

I found a bug, or I have an idea.

Write to hello@playwardlight.com or open an issue on GitHub. It helps to say which device you play on, which land and night you were on, and what happened.
