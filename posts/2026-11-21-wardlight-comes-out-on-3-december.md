Wardlight is on the App Store for pre-order and comes out on Thursday, 3 December.

It is a city builder and a tower defence in one. You are the Wardlight, a flame in the middle of a small village at the edge of a Mist that never ends. You can only build where your light reaches, and your towers can only see lit ground. By day you spend the village's supplies on houses, fields, walls and towers, and the day waits for you as long as you like. When you call the night, the Blight crawl out of the Mist toward whatever burns brightest, and on the hard nights there are a couple of thousand of them alive at once.

A run is twelve nights and takes about forty minutes. The game saves at every dawn, so it splits into short sittings without losing anything.

## What it costs

The download is free, and so is every run with the Ember Attunement, as many as you want. One purchase, the full game, adds the other two Attunements and keeps the Memory (the upgrades between runs) open after your third run. There are no ads, no account and no timers, and the game never needs a network.

A free game with one purchase felt fairer than a paid download: you find out whether you like holding a wall at night before you spend anything.

## Who makes it

Me, Stefan, a software developer in Vienna. Wardlight is a one-person project. The renderer is written for this game in Apple's Metal, because I wanted the Mist to move like weather and the horde to be a real crowd, not a handful of sprites.
